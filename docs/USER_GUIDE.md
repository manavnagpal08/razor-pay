## Step 3: Use Policy-Safe Discounts

You never have to hunt through external coupon websites, but discounts still stay under merchant control.

- BuyFlow shows active store promotions like `WELCOME10`, `SAVE15`, or `FLASH20` when you ask about offers, coupons, or deals.
- Product cards and chat banners can show verified savings after you choose to apply an eligible offer.
- Every coupon is rechecked by the backend Policy Engine before checkout, so the AI cannot exceed merchant discount limits.

## Step 4: 1-Click In-Chat Razorpay Checkout

Buying an item takes just one click directly inside the chat window without leaving the conversation:

1. Click **"Buy Now"** on any recommended item or catalog card.
2. The official **Razorpay Secure Checkout Modal** will pop up instantly.
3. Your **Name**, **Email**, and verified payable amount are already prefilled for you.
4. Choose your payment method:
   - **UPI** (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, QR)
   - **Cards** (Credit / Debit / EMI)
   - **Netbanking** (All Indian Banks)
   - **Wallets / PayLater**
5. Once payment is confirmed, the AI sends a celebration receipt card with your **Razorpay Payment ID** and estimated delivery date!

## Step 3: Setting Up Discount Policies & Safety Guardrails

Under the **Policies** tab, you set the rules for your autonomous store:

| Setting | Purpose | Recommended Value |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Max Discount Limit (%)** | The absolute highest percentage discount the AI can ever offer. | `15% - 20%` |
| **Max Discount Amount (₹)** | Hard cap on total rupee discount per cart. | `₹5,000` |
| **Minimum Order Amount (₹)** | Minimum cart value required before coupons apply. | `₹500` |
| **Auto-Approval Threshold (₹)** | Orders below this amount execute without manual intervention. | `₹2,00,000` |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Financial Guarantee**: Even if a customer tries to prompt the AI to give them a 90% discount, the backend Policy Engine strictly rejects it and clamps the price to your maximum allowed limit.

## Step 4: Configuring Email Delivery (Brevo / Resend)

To send real OTP codes and order receipts to your customers:
1. Open the **Email Delivery** tab in the Merchant Dashboard.
2. Select your provider: **Brevo (Recommended)** or **Resend**.
3. Enter your API Key (e.g., `xkeysib-...` from [brevo.com](https://brevo.com)).
4. Enter your verified Sender Email (e.g., `orders@yourdomain.com`).
5. Click **"Save & Send Test Email"** to verify live inbox delivery.

## Step 6: Connecting External OMS via Webhooks

If you use an external ERP, Shopify, or custom warehouse system:
1. Open the **Webhooks** tab.
2. Enter your endpoint URL: `https://your-api.com/webhooks/orders`.
3. Set your secret key for HMAC-SHA256 signature verification.
4. Toggle **Auto-Sync Orders** on.
5. Every time a customer pays, a signed `order.completed` event is dispatched with customer and shipping details.

# ❓ Part 4: Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

### For Shoppers:

#### 1. Why didn't I receive my 6-digit email OTP?
- Check your **Spam / Junk** folder.
- Ensure you typed your email address correctly without spaces.
- If testing in demo mode, the verification code may be prefilled or logged directly.

#### 2. Are Razorpay payments safe?
- Yes. All transactions use **Razorpay's PCI-DSS Level 1 certified 256-bit encrypted gateway**.
- In test mode, no real money is deducted from your bank account.

#### 3. How do I change the discount coupon?
- If multiple coupons exist, click the **"Apply Code"** button on any offer banner, or type *"Use coupon SAVE15"* in the chat.

---

### For Merchants:

#### 1. How do I share my store link with customers?
- Copy your storefront URL: `https://razorpay-buildthon.vercel.app/chat?merchant=<your_merchant_id>`.
- You can print this as a QR code on table stands, share it on WhatsApp/Instagram, or embed it as an iframe widget on your existing website.

#### 2. How does the system prevent shoppers from tricking the AI into giving free items?
- The AI has **zero authority over payment calculations**.
- Every cart calculation is executed on the Python backend server, and the Policy Engine enforces strict non-negotiable discount ceilings.

#### 3. Can I test live email dispatch without paying for an email service?
- Yes! [Brevo](https://brevo.com) offers 300 free transactional emails per day. Simply generate a free API key, verify your sender email, and paste the key in the **Email Delivery** tab.
